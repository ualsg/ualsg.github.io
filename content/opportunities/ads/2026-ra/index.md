### Job and project description

We are seeking a motivated Research Assistant to join the project “Assessing Urban Flood Resilience against Climate Extreme with GeoAI in Southeast Asia.” The project will develop a GeoAI-based framework for urban flood resilience using multimodal geospatial data, including GIS, remote sensing, street-view imagery, and socio-demographic data in Southeast Asia.

### Scope and responsibilities

Your key responsibilities will be:

- Process, integrate, and analyse multimodal geospatial data.
- Develop and evaluate GeoAI / machine-learning methods for urban flood risk and resilience mapping.
- Conduct spatial analysis, statistical analysis, and visualisation using Python, R, and GIS. Support comparative analyses across Southeast Asian cities.
- Conducting standard research activities, such as literature reviews and contributing to research publications and reports.
- Work closely with the PI, Co-PI, and student researchers, and assisting the team in various activities such as project management, administration, and funding applications.


### Required qualifications

- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Geography, GIScience, Geomatics, Remote Sensing, Computer Science, Data Science, Urban Planning, or a related field from a reputable university.
- Strong GIS and spatial analysis skills, with programming experience in Python and/or R.
- Experience with geospatial data processing and visualization.
- Preferably knowledge of GeoAI, machine learning, deep learning, or computer vision is preferred.
- Experience in urban climate, flooding, disaster risk, remote sensing, or urban analytics is an advantage.
- Strong analytical, communication, and academic writing skills.
- This is an entry-level position, but applicants with some research experience, publications, and evidence such as a developed repository will be prioritized.

Note: Candidates do not need expertise in all areas. Applicants with a strong geospatial foundation and programming skills are encouraged to apply.


### Our offer

- You will be working on a compelling project at the forefront of geographic information modelling, climate resilience, and disaster management.
- Work in a research and educational environment where you may not only contribute to your own personal development, but also to others, especially through collaborations.
- Opportunity to make impactful contributions in the field, on a globally relevant topic, and opportunity to build a research portfolio.
- Visibility and exposure of your work.
- Flexible working environment.
- Exploring new technologies and opportunity to experiment with the state of the art.
- The remuneration will be determined according to the university’s salary range depending on the degree and experience.

### Duration and start

The appointment is for one year. The expected start of the job is in January 2027.


### Location

The main campus of the National University of Singapore. This is not a remote position.
