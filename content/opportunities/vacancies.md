### No suitable opening?

+ **PhD**: outside advertised calls, we accept applicants at both NUS intakes. See the [PhD section of our guide](/opportunities/application-guide/#phd).
+ **Postdocs**: you can join us through an [externally funded fellowship](/opportunities/fellowships/), with us as your host.
+ **Research assistants and postdoc jobs**: these depend on funded projects and are listed above when available. See [what we expect](/opportunities/application-guide/#research-assistants-and-research-fellows-postdocs).
+ **Visiting scholars**: we accept only visitors at PhD level and above. Requests have fixed yearly deadlines and we can host only a few visitors. See [how to propose a visit](/opportunities/application-guide/#visiting-scholars-up-to-12-months).
+ **NUS master students**: please [contact us](/#contact) directly.
+ **Students from outside NUS**: we do not offer internships or thesis supervision to undergraduate or master students who are not studying at our university.
